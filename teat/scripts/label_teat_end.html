Teat-end labelling

Keys

  • Drag: draw a box (several allowed)
  • Enter save boxes & next
  • N no teat end visible & next
  • U unusable photo & next
  • Z undo last box · right-click a box to delete it
  • ← → previous / next (no save)

What to box

  • The teat end: the tip with the opening (orifice) and the whole ring of skin around it.
  • Include the full ring, rough or smooth, plus a small margin. Roughly the last third of the teat; never the whole teat.
  • Same box size whether the ring looks healthy or rough. Don't let the condition change where you draw.
  • One box per teat end you can clearly see. Skip blurry or far-away teat ends.
  • Teat end cut off by the photo edge: box the visible part if the orifice area shows, otherwise N.
  • U only if the photo isn't a teat at all, or is too blurry to place a box.
